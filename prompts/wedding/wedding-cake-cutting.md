# Cake Cutting

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> The bride and groom stand close together behind their wedding cake, hands joined on the cake knife. They press the knife slowly down through the top tier together, smiling at each other, then laugh as the slice leans slightly. Guests are softly blurred in the background, raising glasses. Warm reception lighting, candles glowing on the tables. The camera dollies in slowly toward their joined hands and the cake. Joyful, warm, cinematic reception film look.

## Negative prompt

> hands merging into the knife, cake collapsing, faces swapping, arms stretching, guests' faces distorting, flickering candlelight

## Formula used

Runway image-to-video guidance: one clear **subject action** (pressing the
knife down together), everything else still or softly blurred, camera move
stated once. Keep the prompt short and concrete.

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video (Gen-4)
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9

## Tips

- Hands + an object is a risky combination. If the knife warps, crop the
  photo so the hands and knife fill the frame — larger subjects warp less.
- Two takes minimum: the laugh at the end is the moment to keep, and it
  lands differently every generation.
- Cut in from the toast shot and out to the first dance — the reception
  sequence flows cake, toast, dance.
