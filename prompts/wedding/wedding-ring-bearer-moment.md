# Ring Bearer Moment

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> Low-angle shot at a wedding ceremony. A small ring bearer walks slowly down the aisle carrying a cushion with the rings, concentrating hard, taking careful little steps. Guests smile softly in the blurred background. The cushion stays steady in his hands; the rings glint once in the light. The camera pushes in very slowly from a low angle. Sweet, gentle, cinematic, warm daylight.

## Negative prompt

> child tripping or falling, face morphing, cushion flipping, rings flying off, legs bending wrong, guests' faces distorting

## Formula used

Wan image-to-video: **small subject, small motion, low camera**. Wan is at
its best with one simple action and a near-static frame; a child's careful
walk is exactly that scale.

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video (Wan 2.2)
- Duration: 5 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9

## Tips

- Five seconds is one or two careful steps. That is enough — the audience
  reads the whole moment from it.
- Children warp more than adults in video models because their proportions
  are less common in training data. If the face drifts, use a photo where
  the child is further from the camera.
- Never generate a clip that makes a child stumble or fall, even by
  accident — discard those generations and regenerate.
