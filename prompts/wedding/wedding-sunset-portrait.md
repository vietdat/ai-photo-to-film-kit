# Sunset Portrait

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> A bride and groom stand close together on a hilltop at sunset, holding hands, looking out over a golden valley. The sun sits low behind them, rim-lighting the bride's veil and the groom's shoulders. Her dress moves almost imperceptibly in the evening breeze; he turns his head slightly toward her and smiles. The camera pans slowly to the right, following the light across the landscape. Epic but tender, warm orange and rose tones, cinematic wide shot, film grain.

## Negative prompt

> faces turning into silhouettes, sun flickering, couple sliding, dress morphing into the hillside, lens flare taking over the frame

## Formula used

Kling five-layer structure: **subject** (the couple) + **subject movement**
(her dress stirring, his head turning slightly) + **scene** (hilltop,
golden valley, low sun) + **camera** (slow pan right) + **lighting and
atmosphere** (rim light, warm orange and rose tones, film grain).

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video, first frame = your photo
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Best from a wide photo where the couple is small in frame — the
  landscape does the work and faces stay safe

## Tips

- Wide shots are forgiving: small faces mean fewer face errors. Use them
  for the film's opening and closing.
- If the sun strobes or pulses, add "the sun stays steady" and regenerate.
- Pair with `wedding-sparkler-exit`: sunset portrait opens the film's
  final act, sparkler exit closes it.
