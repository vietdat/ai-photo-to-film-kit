# Night Lights Bokeh

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> Cinematic night portrait. A bride and groom stand close together beneath warm string lights, foreheads almost touching, eyes closed, smiling softly. The lights behind them blur into golden bokeh orbs that drift and shimmer gently. Her veil moves almost imperceptibly in the night air. The camera orbits slowly around the couple, keeping their faces in soft focus. Dreamy, romantic, filmic night photography, warm gold tones, gentle glow.

## Negative prompt

> faces melting together, bokeh turning into hard dots, lights strobing, veil wrapping around faces, camera spinning fast, over-sharpened skin

## Formula used

Veo eight-element structure: **subject** (the couple) + **context** (night,
beneath string lights) + **action** (standing close, foreheads almost
touching) + **style** (dreamy, filmic night photography) + **camera motion**
(slow orbit) + **composition** (close two-shot, bokeh filling the
background) + **ambiance** (warm gold, gentle, romantic) + **audio** (omit
— add music in the edit; see `tutorials/05-music-and-export.md`).

## Settings

- Mode: frames-to-video / image-to-video (Veo 3.1)
- Duration: 8 seconds (this kit standardises on 8 seconds; Veo 3.1 also supports 4s and 6s)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Generate without audio

## Tips

- Bokeh hides background errors beautifully, so this is a safe romantic
  closer — but keep the orbit slow. A fast orbit at night smears faces.
- If the two faces start to merge, add "their faces stay separate, a small
  gap of light between them" and regenerate.
- Works as the last shot before the names card in a reception film.
