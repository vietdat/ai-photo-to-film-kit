# Dance Floor Energy

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> Vertical shot on a wedding dance floor at night. The bride and groom dance in the middle of a laughing crowd, guests' hands raised, warm string lights and soft disco light washing over the scene. The couple spins together, laughing, as the crowd sways around them. The camera holds a gentle handheld sway, staying with the couple, never rushing. Joyful, warm, slightly grainy party film look, motion feels alive but readable.

## Negative prompt

> faces smearing, crowd turning into a blur of limbs, arms stretching, strobe flashing, camera whipping around, people duplicating

## Formula used

Runway image-to-video guidance: describe the **energy in one sentence**
(a laughing crowd swaying around a dancing couple), keep the camera move
small and human (a gentle sway, not a whip-pan), and let the lights do
the motion.

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video (Gen-4)
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (vertical — this is a social cut, not the main film)
- Keep the prompt under 120 words

## Tips

- Crowds are the hardest subject for video models. If the crowd smears,
  crop the photo to the couple plus two or three nearby guests.
- A little motion blur is your friend here — it hides small errors and
  reads as energy. Total sharpness reads as fake.
- Use this as a 10-second vertical cut for socials, not inside the 60-second
  reception film — the film stays 16:9 throughout.
