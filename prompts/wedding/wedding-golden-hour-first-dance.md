# Golden Hour First Dance

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> A cinematic slow-motion shot of a bride and groom sharing their first dance in an open field at golden hour. Warm low sun flares through the trees behind them. The groom gently spins the bride once; her dress and veil drift outward with the turn, then settle as they pull close again. Leaves drift softly in the background. The camera dollies in slowly and orbits slightly to the left, keeping the couple centred. Film grain, warm amber tones, shallow depth of field, 35mm cinema look, romantic and tender mood.

## Negative prompt

> distorted faces, extra fingers, warped hands, morphing dress, flickering light, sudden camera jumps, cartoon look, oversaturated colours, text, watermark

## Formula used

Kling five-layer structure: **subject** (bride and groom) + **subject movement**
(the spin, the dress drifting) + **scene** (open field at golden hour) +
**camera** (slow dolly-in, slight left orbit) + **lighting and atmosphere**
(warm sun flare, film grain, amber tones).

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video, first frame = your photo
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (use 9:16 only if the source photo is vertical)
- Creativity/relevance: balanced — keep the slider near the middle so the
  couple's faces stay faithful to the photo
- Generate 2–3 takes and keep the one where the spin is smoothest

## Tips

- Works best on photos where both faces are visible and the couple fills at
  least half the frame.
- If the veil warps, shorten the prompt to a single turn instead of a spin,
  or regenerate with the "relevance" setting raised.
- For a reception film, place this clip in the middle of the film, not at
  the start — it is the emotional peak.
