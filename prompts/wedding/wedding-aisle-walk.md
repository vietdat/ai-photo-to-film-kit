# Aisle Walk

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> The bride walks slowly down the ceremony aisle on her father's arm, guests standing and smiling on both sides. Her veil trails gently behind her; flower petals scattered on the aisle shift slightly as she passes. She looks ahead, smiling, then glances briefly toward the groom waiting at the altar. The camera tracks backward smoothly ahead of her, keeping her centred, as the altar and floral arch grow slowly larger in the background. Soft daylight, cinematic wedding film, steady and graceful pace.

## Negative prompt

> faces morphing, guests' faces distorting, bride sliding instead of walking, legs bending wrong, camera wobble, people appearing or disappearing

## Formula used

Kling five-layer structure: **subject** (bride and father) + **subject
movement** (slow walk, veil trailing) + **scene** (aisle, guests, floral
arch) + **camera** (smooth backward track) + **lighting and atmosphere**
(soft daylight, graceful pace).

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video, first frame = your photo
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- If the source photo is taken from the front, the backward track reads
  naturally; from the side, ask for a slow lateral track instead

## Tips

- Walking is hard for video models. Keep the walk slow and the distance
  short — a few steps, not the whole aisle.
- If legs glitch, regenerate with "she takes slow, small steps" and raise
  relevance.
- Cut this clip on the moment she glances at the groom — it is the natural
  edit point into the vows shot.
