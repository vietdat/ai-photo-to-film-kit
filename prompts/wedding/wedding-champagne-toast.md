# Champagne Toast

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> Medium shot at a wedding reception table, golden hour light through the windows. The bride and groom raise champagne flutes toward each other, glasses catching the light, tiny bubbles rising inside. They clink gently, smile, and take a small sip. Candlelight flickers softly on the table; guests are warmly blurred behind them. The camera pushes in slowly past the glasses toward the couple. Elegant, warm, cinematic, natural glass reflections, no exaggerated sparkle.

## Negative prompt

> glasses bending or merging, liquid spilling upward, hands passing through glasses, faces warping, candles flaring, strobing light

## Formula used

Veo eight-element structure: **subject** (bride and groom) + **context**
(reception table, golden hour) + **action** (raising and clinking flutes)
+ **style** (elegant, cinematic) + **camera motion** (slow push-in past
the glasses) + **composition** (medium shot, glasses in the foreground,
couple behind) + **ambiance** (warm, candlelit, joyful) + **audio** (omit
— add music in the edit; see `tutorials/05-music-and-export.md`).

## Settings

- Mode: frames-to-video / image-to-video (Veo 3.1)
- Duration: 8 seconds (this kit standardises on 8 seconds; Veo 3.1 also supports 4s and 6s)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Generate without audio

## Tips

- Glass is a known weak spot: transparent objects warp easily. Keep the
  glasses large in frame and the motion slow.
- If a flute bends, crop the photo tighter on the couple and let the
  glasses sit at the frame edge.
- The clink is the beat to cut on — end the clip just after the glasses
  touch.
