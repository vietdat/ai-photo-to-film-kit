# Rings Close-Up

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> Extreme close-up of a bride and groom's hands resting together, wedding bands catching the light. A single soft glint travels slowly across the gold band as the light shifts. The bouquet beneath stays still; one small petal trembles almost imperceptibly. The camera pushes in very slowly, almost static. Macro lens look, shallow depth of field, warm soft light, quiet and intimate mood.

## Negative prompt

> fingers bending, rings sliding off, hands morphing, extra fingers, petals falling in clumps, camera shake

## Formula used

Wan image-to-video: short, concrete motion verbs and a **near-static camera**.
Wan is strongest when the motion is small and the subject is large in frame;
tiny movements (a glint, a trembling petal) read as real, while big gestures
often warp hands.

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video (Wan 2.2)
- Duration: 5 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- This is a 5-second detail shot — do not stretch it to 10 seconds, the
  hands will start to drift

## Tips

- Hands are the hardest thing for video models. Keep the clip short and
  the motion tiny.
- If a finger bends wrong, crop the photo tighter before generating, or
  pick a different ring photo.
- In the edit, hold this shot for its full 5 seconds and let the music
  breathe — it is a pause, not a transition.
