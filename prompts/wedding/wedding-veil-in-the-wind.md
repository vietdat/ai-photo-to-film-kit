# Veil in the Wind

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> A bride stands in soft evening light, her long veil lifting and rippling in a gentle breeze. The veil floats up, catches the light, and drifts back down in slow, graceful waves. Her hair moves slightly; she holds her bouquet still and looks toward the horizon with a soft smile. The camera orbits slowly around her from a low angle, rising a little as it moves. Dreamy, airy, cinematic wedding film look, soft highlights, natural skin texture.

## Negative prompt

> veil turning into fabric blobs, face changing, hair morphing into the veil, extra arms, jitter, fast movement, harsh shadows

## Formula used

Runway image-to-video guidance: describe the **subject and the one motion
that matters** (the veil), keep everything else still, and state the camera
move once, plainly. Runway follows simple, specific motion descriptions
better than long lists of effects.

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video (Gen-4)
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Keep the prompt under 120 words — Runway truncates very long prompts

## Tips

- One motion only. If you also ask for hair, dress, and background motion,
  the veil — the point of the shot — gets weaker.
- If the veil stretches unnaturally, regenerate with "the veil stays
  attached at the headpiece" added to the prompt.
- Pairs well with `wedding-golden-hour-first-dance` as the shot before it.
