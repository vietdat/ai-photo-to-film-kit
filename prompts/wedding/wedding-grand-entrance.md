# Grand Entrance

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> High crane shot over a wedding reception hall. The doors open and the bride and groom enter together to applause, walking slowly down a path between the tables toward the dance floor. Guests rise, clap, and cheer; warm light washes over the room and the floral decorations. The couple waves once, smiling, and keeps walking. The camera descends slowly from high above, following them down toward the dance floor. Grand, joyful, cinematic, wide establishing shot.

## Negative prompt

> guests' faces distorting, couple sliding instead of walking, arms stretching, people duplicating, camera dropping too fast, lights flickering

## Formula used

Kling five-layer structure: **subject** (the couple entering) + **subject
movement** (walking slowly, one wave) + **scene** (reception hall, tables,
guests rising) + **camera** (high crane descending slowly) + **lighting and
atmosphere** (warm wash of light, grand and joyful).

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video, first frame = your photo
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Works best from a wide, high photo of the room — the couple can be small
  in frame

## Tips

- Wide and high is forgiving: small faces, big room, and the motion is
  mostly the camera, not the people.
- If the couple glides instead of walking, add "they take slow, visible
  steps" and raise relevance.
- This is a natural second shot in a reception film — after the quiet
  morning opener, the entrance raises the energy.
