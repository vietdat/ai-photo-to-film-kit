# Morning Getting Ready

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> A quiet wedding morning. The bride sits by a bright window as her mother fastens the last button of her dress. Soft daylight falls across the room; the bouquet rests on the bed beside them, and the veil hangs nearby, swaying almost imperceptibly. The bride looks down and smiles softly. The camera dollies in slowly from the doorway, unhurried. Tender, calm, documentary wedding film look, natural light, muted warm tones.

## Negative prompt

> hands morphing, buttons multiplying, faces changing, mother appearing or disappearing, curtains flapping wildly, camera rushing in

## Formula used

Kling five-layer structure: **subject** (bride and mother) + **subject
movement** (fastening a button, a soft smile) + **scene** (bright morning
room, bouquet, veil) + **camera** (slow dolly-in from the doorway) +
**lighting and atmosphere** (soft daylight, calm, tender).

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video, first frame = your photo
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9

## Tips

- This is the film's opening shot: quiet before the ceremony. Slow is
  correct — do not speed it up in the edit.
- Hands near a dress are risky. If the buttoning warps, reframe the prompt
  to "her mother adjusts the veil" — one hand, one motion.
- Keep the colour grade muted and warm so it matches the ceremony shots
  that follow.
