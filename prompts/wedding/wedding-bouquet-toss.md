# Bouquet Toss

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> The bride, back to camera, tosses her bouquet high into the air with both hands. The bouquet arcs upward, petals scattering lightly, while a group of friends below reach up laughing, hands open. The bouquet hangs for a moment at the top of its arc. Locked-off static camera, bright daylight, joyful energy, crisp and clean motion, wedding film look.

## Negative prompt

> bouquet multiplying, arms stretching, hands morphing, bouquet teleporting, people sliding across the frame, motion blur overload

## Formula used

Wan image-to-video: one **fast, simple arc** of motion, locked-off camera.
Wan handles a single clear trajectory better than a moving camera plus a
moving subject at the same time.

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video (Wan 2.2)
- Duration: 5 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- A locked-off camera is a feature here, not a limitation — it keeps the
  bouquet's arc readable

## Tips

- Five seconds is exactly one toss. Do not ask for the catch as well;
  generate the catch as a second clip from a different photo if you need it.
- If the bouquet duplicates mid-air, add "a single bouquet" to the prompt
  and regenerate.
- This is a high-energy beat — place it between two slow shots so the film
  breathes around it.
