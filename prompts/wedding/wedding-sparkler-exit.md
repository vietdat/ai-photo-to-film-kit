# Sparkler Exit

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> Cinematic wide shot, night. A newly married couple walks hand in hand down a path lined with guests holding sparklers. Golden sparks trail and flicker in the dark, lighting the couple's smiling faces in warm flashes. The bride's veil lifts slightly in the night air. The camera cranes up and pulls back slowly, revealing more of the glowing crowd and the string lights above. Romantic, joyful, filmic night photography, natural sparkler glow, no artificial lens flare overload.

## Negative prompt

> faces melting in the dark, sparklers turning into fireballs, guests' arms stretching, strobing light, video noise, cartoon sparks

## Formula used

Veo eight-element structure: **subject** (the couple) + **context** (night
sparkler exit) + **action** (walking hand in hand) + **style** (cinematic,
filmic night photography) + **camera motion** (slow crane up and back) +
**composition** (wide shot, couple centred, crowd framing) + **ambiance**
(warm sparkler glow, joyful) + **audio** (omit audio — this kit generates
silent clips and adds music in the edit; see `tutorials/05-music-and-export.md`).

## Settings

- Mode: frames-to-video / image-to-video (Veo 3.1)
- Duration: 8 seconds (this kit standardises on 8 seconds; Veo 3.1 also supports 4s and 6s)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Generate without audio, or with audio muted — add your licensed music
  track in the edit instead

## Tips

- Night shots hide small face errors, which makes this a forgiving final
  shot — but sparklers are easy to overdo. "Flicker" beats "explode".
- This is the classic closing shot of a reception film: put it last, hold
  the final frame for a beat, then fade to the couple's names.
- If the couple's faces soften too much in the dark, use a photo where
  their faces are already lit by the sparklers.
