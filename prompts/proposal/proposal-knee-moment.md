# The Knee Moment

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> A man kneels on one knee before his partner in a quiet park, holding open a small ring box. She stands before him, both hands covering her mouth, eyes wide with surprise, then breaking into a joyful smile. He looks up at her, smiling nervously. Leaves drift slowly around them; the light is soft and golden. The camera pushes in slowly, holding on her reaction. Tender, real, cinematic, documentary feel, natural light.

## Negative prompt

> ring box snapping shut, man standing up suddenly, her hands morphing into her face, faces swapping, people walking through the frame, camera rushing in

## Formula used

Kling five-layer structure: **subject** (the couple) + **subject movement**
(hands covering her mouth, then a smile breaking through) + **scene**
(quiet park, drifting leaves) + **camera** (slow push-in on her reaction)
+ **lighting and atmosphere** (soft golden light, tender, real).

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video, first frame = your photo
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- This is the shot the whole film is about — generate 3–4 takes and keep
  the best reaction

## Tips

- Her reaction is the film. If her hands merge with her face, regenerate
  with "her hands stay below her chin" added.
- Keep the moment secret in real life: generate from a staged rehearsal
  photo or a photo taken after the fact, and show the film only after the
  real proposal. See `tutorials/02-storyboard.md` for how to plan this
  without spoiling the surprise.
- The push-in should be slow. A fast push-in turns a tender moment into
  a jump scare.
