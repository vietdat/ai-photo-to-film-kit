# City Lights Proposal

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> A couple on a rooftop at night, the city lights glittering behind them. He kneels on one knee, holding the ring toward her; she looks down at him, laughing through tears, one hand reaching toward him. The city lights shimmer gently in the background, a few car headlights drifting far below. The camera pushes in slowly past the lights toward the couple. Romantic, cinematic, warm and electric, night photography with soft glow.

## Negative prompt

> faces melting in the dark, city lights strobing, ring glowing unnaturally, her hand stretching, camera shaking, people appearing on the rooftop

## Formula used

Runway image-to-video guidance: one clear **subject action** (kneeling,
reaching), a still city behind them, camera move stated once. Night hides
small errors — use that.

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video (Gen-4)
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Keep the prompt under 120 words

## Tips

- Night shots are forgiving on faces, but the ring is the risk: a tiny
  bright object that models love to exaggerate. Keep the ring small in
  frame.
- If her reaching hand stretches, crop the photo so her hand is already
  close to him.
- The city bokeh behind them is doing the emotional work — keep the couple
  in soft focus rather than razor sharp.
