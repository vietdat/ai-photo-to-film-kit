# She Said Yes

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> The moment after the proposal. She nods yes, laughing, and throws her arms around him; he lifts her slightly off the ground in a joyful embrace, spinning her once, slowly. Her hair lifts with the spin and settles as he sets her down. They hold each other, foreheads together, laughing. The camera cranes up slowly as they embrace, opening up to the sky above them. Joyful, bright, cinematic, the happiest ten seconds of the film.

## Negative prompt

> faces merging during the embrace, arms stretching, her body lifting unnaturally high, hair morphing into the sky, camera rising too fast, people appearing out of nowhere

## Formula used

Kling five-layer structure: **subject** (the couple) + **subject movement**
(the embrace, one slow spin, foreheads together) + **scene** (wherever the
proposal happened — keep it the same as the previous shot) + **camera**
(slow crane up) + **lighting and atmosphere** (bright, joyful).

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video, first frame = your photo
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Generate 3–4 takes: the lift-and-spin is the hardest motion in this kit

## Tips

- The lift is the risk. If he lifts her too high or she floats, change
  the prompt to a standing embrace with no lift — still joyful, far safer.
- Keep the location identical to the previous shot (same park, same beach)
  so the two clips cut together seamlessly.
- This is the last shot of the proposal film. Hold the final frame, then
  fade to the couple's names and the date.
