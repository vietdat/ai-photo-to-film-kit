# Under String Lights

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> A couple stands in a garden at dusk beneath warm string lights. He takes both her hands, kneels slowly onto one knee, and looks up at her; she gasps softly, smiling, as the lights glow warm around them. Fireflies drift in the dark garden behind them. The camera dollies in slowly through the lights toward the couple. Warm, magical, cinematic, soft golden bokeh, gentle evening air.

## Negative prompt

> faces morphing, string lights strobing, fireflies turning into sparks, man dropping too fast, her hands stretching, camera pushing through the lights too quickly

## Formula used

Runway image-to-video guidance: one **slow, deliberate action** (kneeling),
a warm still setting, camera move stated once. Let the lights and the
fireflies carry the magic — the people just need to be still and real.

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video (Gen-4)
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Keep the prompt under 120 words

## Tips

- The kneel should take the full ten seconds. If he drops too fast, add
  "he lowers himself very slowly" and regenerate.
- Fireflies are a gamble — if they turn into sparks or dust, drop them
  from the prompt. The string lights alone are enough.
- This shot works as the proposal film's opener: lights, garden, then the
  question.
