# Mountain Top Proposal

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> Cinematic wide shot on a mountain summit at sunrise. A man kneels on one knee before his partner on a rocky outcrop, holding the ring box open, mountain ridges and low clouds stretching behind them. Her jacket flutters gently in the wind; she covers her mouth with both hands, laughing. The camera orbits slowly around the couple, the mountains turning behind them. Epic, joyful, filmic, crisp morning light, natural wind motion.

## Negative prompt

> couple sliding off the rock, mountains warping, clouds rushing past, jacket flapping wildly, faces turning into silhouettes, camera spinning

## Formula used

Veo eight-element structure: **subject** (the couple) + **context**
(mountain summit at sunrise) + **action** (kneeling, hands covering her
mouth) + **style** (cinematic, epic, filmic) + **camera motion** (slow
orbit) + **composition** (wide shot, couple centred, ridges behind) +
**ambiance** (crisp, joyful, morning light) + **audio** (omit — add music
in the edit; see `tutorials/05-music-and-export.md`).

## Settings

- Mode: frames-to-video / image-to-video (Veo 3.1)
- Duration: 8 seconds (this kit standardises on 8 seconds; Veo 3.1 also supports 4s and 6s)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Generate without audio

## Tips

- Wide and epic is forgiving — the couple is small and the mountains do
  the work.
- Wind is the risk: a fluttering jacket is lovely, a flapping one is a
  glitch. Add "the jacket moves only slightly" if it flaps.
- This is the proposal film's hero shot. Put it in the middle, not at the
  start — the hike up is the build-up.
