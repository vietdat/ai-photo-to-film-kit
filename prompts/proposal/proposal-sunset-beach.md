# Sunset Beach Proposal

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> Cinematic wide shot on a beach at sunset. A man kneels before his partner at the water's edge, the ring box open in his hand. She stands with her hands to her face in surprise as gentle waves roll in behind them, catching the orange light. Her dress moves softly in the sea breeze. The camera cranes up and pulls back slowly, revealing more of the glowing shoreline. Romantic, epic, filmic, warm sunset tones, natural wave motion.

## Negative prompt

> waves freezing or reversing, couple sliding on the sand, faces turning into silhouettes, sun flickering, ring box disappearing, camera rising too fast

## Formula used

Veo eight-element structure: **subject** (the couple) + **context**
(beach at sunset, water's edge) + **action** (kneeling, hands to her
face) + **style** (cinematic, filmic, epic) + **camera motion** (slow crane
up and back) + **composition** (wide shot, couple small against the
shoreline) + **ambiance** (warm sunset, romantic) + **audio** (omit — add
music in the edit; see `tutorials/05-music-and-export.md`).

## Settings

- Mode: frames-to-video / image-to-video (Veo 3.1)
- Duration: 8 seconds (this kit standardises on 8 seconds; Veo 3.1 also supports 4s and 6s)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Generate without audio

## Tips

- Wide shots are forgiving: the couple is small, the beach does the work.
- Waves are a known trouble spot — if they reverse or freeze, add "waves
  roll gently toward the shore" and regenerate.
- This is a strong closing shot for a proposal film: the question asked,
  the beach glowing, the camera rising.
