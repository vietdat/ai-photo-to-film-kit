# Ring Reveal

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> Extreme close-up of an open ring box in a man's hand, the diamond catching the light and throwing a tiny rainbow glint across his fingers. The box stays steady; the glint moves slowly as the light shifts. The camera pushes in very slowly on the ring, then tilts up slightly to reveal her smiling face, softly out of focus behind it. Intimate, sparkling, cinematic macro look, shallow depth of field, warm light.

## Negative prompt

> ring multiplying, diamond turning into a blob, fingers bending, box snapping shut, face morphing into focus too fast, glint strobing

## Formula used

Kling five-layer structure: **subject** (the ring box) + **subject movement**
(the glint travelling slowly) + **scene** (his hand, her soft face behind)
+ **camera** (very slow push-in, slight tilt up) + **lighting and
atmosphere** (warm light, intimate, sparkling).

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video, first frame = your photo
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Keep the ring large in frame — small bright objects warp first

## Tips

- The glint is the only motion that matters. Everything else should be
  still.
- If the diamond blobs, regenerate with "the diamond stays a single
  faceted stone" added.
- In the edit, cut to this shot right after the knee moment — question,
  then ring, then her answer.
