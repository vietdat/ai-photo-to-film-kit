# Vow Renewal

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> A couple renew their vows in a small garden ceremony, years after their wedding. They face each other holding hands, older now, smiling through happy tears. She squeezes his hands once; he nods slowly. Petals drift down around them from the trees above. The camera pushes in slowly, then drifts gently sideways, keeping their joined hands in frame. Soft daylight, cinematic, warm and hopeful, film grain.

## Negative prompt

> faces morphing, hands merging, petals turning into snow, tears streaming unnaturally, guests' faces distorting, camera wobble

## Formula used

Kling five-layer structure: **subject** (the couple) + **subject movement**
(a hand squeeze, a slow nod) + **scene** (garden ceremony, drifting
petals) + **camera** (slow push-in, gentle lateral drift) + **lighting and
atmosphere** (soft daylight, warm and hopeful).

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video, first frame = your photo
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9

## Tips

- The hand squeeze is the beat. If the hands merge, crop tighter on the
  faces and let the hands sit lower in frame.
- Older faces are actually easier for video models than young ones —
  wrinkles and texture give the model more to hold onto.
- Place this as the climax of the anniversary film, just before the final
  portrait.
