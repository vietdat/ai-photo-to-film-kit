# Photo Album Coming Alive

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> A close shot of an open photo album on a wooden table, warm lamplight. The photograph on the page — a young couple on their wedding day — comes gently alive: the bride's veil lifts a fraction in a breeze that isn't there, the groom smiles a little wider, light moves softly across the old print. The album, the table, and the hands holding the page stay completely still. Locked-off static camera. Nostalgic, tender, magical-realist, soft warm light, gentle film grain.

## Negative prompt

> photo tearing, people climbing out of the photo, page flipping on its own, hands morphing, the print warping off the page, flicker

## Formula used

Runway image-to-video guidance: **one impossible motion, everything else
frozen**. The contrast between the still album and the faintly moving
photograph is what sells the magic. State the stillness explicitly.

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video (Gen-4)
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9

## Tips

- Photograph the album page straight down, in warm light, with no glare —
  glare becomes moving artefacts in the video.
- Keep the "alive" motion tiny. A veil lifting a fraction reads as magic;
  a full dance reads as a mistake.
- This is the emotional centre of an anniversary film: place it in the
  middle, after the toast and before the dance.
