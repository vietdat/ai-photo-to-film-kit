# Golden Hour Toast

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> A couple celebrating their anniversary on a terrace at golden hour raise their glasses toward each other, smiling the easy smile of people who have done this many times before. Warm low sun catches the rims of the glasses and the lines around their eyes. A sheer curtain drifts gently behind them. The camera dollies in slowly and settles on the couple. Warm, relaxed, cinematic, film grain, amber tones, a lifetime in one look.

## Negative prompt

> faces morphing, glasses bending, liquid spilling, hands passing through glasses, curtain flapping wildly, camera rushing

## Formula used

Kling five-layer structure: **subject** (the couple) + **subject movement**
(raising glasses, an easy smile) + **scene** (terrace at golden hour) +
**camera** (slow dolly-in, settling) + **lighting and atmosphere** (warm
low sun, relaxed, amber tones).

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video, first frame = your photo
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9

## Tips

- Anniversary films work best when the couple looks relaxed, not posed.
  Pick a candid photo, not a stiff portrait.
- If the glasses warp, crop tighter on the faces and let the glasses sit
  low in frame.
- This is a strong opening shot for a 30-second anniversary film.
