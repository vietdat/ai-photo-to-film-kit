# Candlelit Dinner

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> A couple shares a candlelit anniversary dinner at a small table. Two candles flicker gently between them, their flames bending slightly in the still air. She reaches across the table and takes his hand; he smiles and turns his glass slowly in his fingers. The restaurant is a warm blur behind them. The camera pushes in slowly across the table toward the couple. Intimate, warm, cinematic, soft candlelight, shallow depth of field.

## Negative prompt

> flames flaring or going out, hands merging, glasses bending, faces morphing, candles melting fast, background people walking through the frame

## Formula used

Kling five-layer structure: **subject** (the couple) + **subject movement**
(reaching across the table, turning a glass) + **scene** (candlelit table,
warm blurred restaurant) + **camera** (slow push-in across the table) +
**lighting and atmosphere** (soft candlelight, intimate, warm).

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video, first frame = your photo
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9

## Tips

- Candle flames should bend, not flare. If they flare, add "the flames
  stay small and steady" and regenerate.
- Two motions maximum (the reach, the glass). If you add a third — a head
  turn, a smile — one of them will break.
- This shot pairs with the golden-hour toast: dinner inside, toast
  outside, same evening.
