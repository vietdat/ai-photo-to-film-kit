# Fifty Years Later

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> Cinematic shot on a house porch at late afternoon. An elderly couple sits close together on a wooden bench, her head resting on his shoulder, his arm around her, both looking out at their garden. A wind chime sways gently; leaves drift across the lawn. Neither of them speaks — they just sit, comfortable in fifty years of quiet. The camera dollies in slowly. Warm, golden, nostalgic, filmic, natural light, gentle and unhurried.

## Negative prompt

> faces morphing, couple standing up suddenly, bench sliding, wind chime swinging wildly, leaves turning into a storm, camera rushing

## Formula used

Veo eight-element structure: **subject** (the elderly couple) + **context**
(porch, late afternoon, fiftieth anniversary) + **action** (sitting close,
looking at the garden) + **style** (cinematic, nostalgic, filmic) +
**camera motion** (slow dolly-in) + **composition** (medium-wide two-shot,
garden behind) + **ambiance** (warm, golden, quiet) + **audio** (omit —
add music in the edit; see `tutorials/05-music-and-export.md`).

## Settings

- Mode: frames-to-video / image-to-video (Veo 3.1)
- Duration: 8 seconds (this kit standardises on 8 seconds; Veo 3.1 also supports 4s and 6s)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Generate without audio

## Tips

- Stillness is the point. If the model adds motion, it is wrong — ask for
  less, not more.
- Use this as the closing shot of a milestone anniversary film, holding
  the final frame for two extra seconds under the music.
- If the faces soften too much, use a photo taken closer to the couple.
