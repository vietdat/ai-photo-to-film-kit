# Dance in the Kitchen

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> A couple slow-dances in their kitchen in the evening, no music visible, just the two of them. She rests her head on his shoulder; he sways gently from side to side, one hand on her back. Warm light from the kitchen lamp, dishes drying on the rack behind them, completely ordinary and completely romantic. Locked-off static camera. Tender, quiet, documentary feel, natural warm light.

## Negative prompt

> couple spinning fast, feet sliding, arms stretching, faces morphing, kitchen objects moving on their own, camera shake

## Formula used

Wan image-to-video: **one slow sway, locked-off camera, small frame**.
Domestic scenes work in Wan because the motion is tiny and the setting is
ordinary — there is nothing complex to warp.

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video (Wan 2.2)
- Duration: 5 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9

## Tips

- Five seconds of a slow sway is the whole shot. Do not ask for a spin.
- The charm is the ordinariness — dishes, lamplight, a small kitchen.
  Do not dress it up.
- Cut this right after the photo-album shot: past (the album) into
  present (the kitchen).
