# Slow Waltz in the Living Room

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> A couple slow-waltzes in their living room in the evening, framed photos on the walls behind them, a lamp glowing warm in the corner. They turn together slowly, one small step at a time, her dress swaying gently with each turn. The room is still except for them. The camera orbits slowly around the couple at chest height, keeping them centred. Warm, intimate, cinematic, soft lamplight, gentle film grain.

## Negative prompt

> couple spinning fast, feet sliding across the floor, dress morphing, furniture moving, faces swapping, camera orbiting too fast

## Formula used

Runway image-to-video guidance: one **slow, repeated motion** (the waltz
turn), a simple camera move, and a still room. The room's stillness makes
the turn readable.

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video (Gen-4)
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Keep the prompt under 120 words

## Tips

- A waltz is a turn, not a spin. If the model spins them, add "one slow
  turn, taking the full ten seconds" and regenerate.
- Feet are the risk. Keep the camera at chest height so the feet are small
  in frame or out of it.
- The framed photos on the wall are a nice touch — they echo the couple's
  history without needing a flashback.
