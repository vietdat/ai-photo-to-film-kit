# Emotional Close-Up

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> An intimate close-up of the subject of this photograph. Their expression shifts almost imperceptibly — a slow blink, a breath, the smallest softening of a smile, eyes glistening slightly as if holding back happy tears. Nothing else moves. The background is soft and out of focus. The camera is almost static, pushing in so slowly it is barely felt. Soft natural light, natural skin texture, quiet documentary feel, deeply human.

## Negative prompt

> lips moving out of sync, teeth warping, face morphing, eyes drifting, exaggerated crying, beauty-filter skin, camera shake

## Formula used

Kling five-layer structure: **subject** (the face in your photo) +
**subject movement** (a slow blink, a breath, a softening smile) + **scene**
(soft out-of-focus background) + **camera** (near-static, imperceptible
push-in) + **lighting and atmosphere** (soft natural light, quiet,
documentary).

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video, first frame = your photo
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (or 9:16 for a vertical close-up)

## Tips

- The smaller the movement, the safer the face. A blink and a breath are
  enough — the audience projects the emotion.
- Do not ask for lip-sync or specific words. See
  `tutorials/06-troubleshooting.md` for why.
- If the eyes drift, regenerate with "their gaze stays fixed" added.
- This is the most powerful shot in any film and the easiest to break.
  Generate four takes and keep the best.
