# Cinematic Push-In

## The prompt (copy-paste)

> A slow, steady cinematic push-in on the subject of this photograph. The subject stays natural and still, with only the smallest hint of life — a breath, a blink, a slight shift of expression. The background stays soft and still. Warm natural light, shallow depth of field, film grain, quiet and intimate mood. The camera moves in slowly and never rushes, as if the viewer is being invited closer.

## Negative prompt

> subject morphing, face changing, sudden movement, camera shaking, background people moving, flickering light

## Formula used

Kling five-layer structure: **subject** (whoever is in your photo) +
**subject movement** (almost none — a breath, a blink) + **scene** (your
photo's setting) + **camera** (slow steady push-in) + **lighting and
atmosphere** (warm natural light, quiet, intimate).

## Settings

- Mode: image-to-video, first frame = your photo
- Duration: 10 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (or 9:16 for a vertical photo)

## Tips

- This is the safest prompt in the kit. When a fancier prompt fails three
  times, fall back to this one — a slow push-in on a good photo almost
  always works.
- Adapt it by replacing "the subject" with a plain description of who is
  in the photo: "the bride and groom", "the elderly couple", "the man
  kneeling".
- Use it as the opening or closing shot of any film: it establishes the
  people before the story starts.
