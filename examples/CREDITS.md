# Photo credits

All sample photos in `examples/sample-album/` are free-license stock
photos downloaded from Unsplash and used under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), which allows free use
without attribution. They are credited here anyway.

**Important:** these are stock photos, not real client weddings. They are
stand-ins so the example film and storyboards can be built and tested.
Replace them with your own photos for real work. No photo in this repo
is presented as a real client's wedding.

Photographer names could not be verified from the Unsplash CDN URLs
alone, so each photo is credited by its source URL rather than by a name
we could not confirm. If you know a photographer for one of these
photos, please open a pull request to add the name here.
